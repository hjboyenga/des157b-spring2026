Hello — thanks for being here.

You're one of the first people to play Now What? We'd love your honest impressions. As you go, keep these three things in mind:

  1. 01
    Play all the way through at least once. Choose the path that feels most true to you — don't second-guess yourself.
  2. 02
    Notice where you feel confused or stuck. If a question's wording trips you up, or a transition feels abrupt, that's exactly what we want to know about.
  3. 03
    Try a second path using "Try a different path." See if the ending you reach feels distinct and whether the chart at the end loads correctly.

Your responses are recorded anonymously. There are no wrong answers.
