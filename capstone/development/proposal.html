Capstone Proposal

My capstone project will center around life after college, specifically as a design major. It will be a choose-your-own-adventure game, and the questions will change based on the user's answers. For example, if you choose a series of questions and answers, the game will ask more questions regarding those choices. Ending with a result of how your career might go based on the choices you made. I would like this to be a reflection on how we are all asking questions about our future, and get a glimpse of how someone can navigate these difficult times. Another idea was to include a way to display the results, showing the percentage of people who chose a similar path. I don't know exactly how I would do that, but it might be something that I would want to add.

The main audience for this project is my classmates and other graduating students, since I think a lot of us ask similar questions about post-college life. Some of the mental models I want to include are the scarcity mindset, which surrounds believing there are limited opportunities that exist post-college. Also, I think it is important to highlight that the assumption that a linear career path is the only way to success is wrong.

Overall, I believe this project will have a minimalist approach, and it has different elements coming forward and back. Each question will probably have a small illustration highlighting the major themes from the questions. I think the main goal will be to have a lot of different questions that can branch off from one another.
